BUILD YOUR OWN ARC (100% FREE VERSION)
Talk to Arc from your laptop or phone, and your laptop does what you ask.
NO PAID APIs NEEDED. NO TELECOM CREDITS. ZERO COST.

BEFORE YOU START:
  - Just a Windows laptop.
That's it! Everything else installs automatically.


=====================================================================
 HOW TO START (Takes 2 minutes)
=====================================================================

1. Double-click  Start-Arc.bat
   If Windows asks "Do you want to allow this app...", click Yes.
   The first time it installs what it needs (Node.js & cloudflared).

2. It will ask for your mobile number (example: 919876543210).
   Type it and press Enter.

3. Optional: It will ask for a free Google Gemini API key.
   - If you have one from https://aistudio.google.com/ , paste it and press Enter.
   - Or just press Enter to skip! Arc has a built-in smart engine that
     works 100% offline with zero keys.

4. Wait until the window says:
   ⚡ ARC IS READY (100% FREE VERSION)
   Keep that black window open. (Closing it switches Arc off.)


=====================================================================
 HOW TO TALK TO ARC (FREE VOICE CALL)
=====================================================================

On your laptop:
  1. Open http://localhost:3710 in your browser (Chrome or Edge).
  2. Click the green "📞 Call Arc" button.
  3. Arc will say: "Hey Boss, it's Arc. What can I do for you?"
  4. Talk into your microphone!

On your phone (from anywhere):
  1. Look at the Start-Arc window: it shows a free Cloudflare link
     (e.g., https://something.trycloudflare.com).
     It is already copied to your clipboard!
  2. Open that link on your phone's browser (Chrome or Safari).
  3. Tap "📞 Call Arc" and talk to your laptop from your phone!
